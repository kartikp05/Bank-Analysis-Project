### Problem statement
- In order to ascertain the bank's performance lending activities and performance, we need to create a comprehensive bank loan report. 
- We need a report that aims to provide insights into key loan-related metrics and their changes over time. 
- The report will help the business make data-driven decisions, track our loan portfolio's health, and identify trends that can inform its lending strategies.
### The Data
KPIs to consider:
1. Total Loan Applications
2. Total Funded Amount
3. Total Amount Received
4. Average Interest Rate
5. Average Debt-To-Income Ratio
- What's a "Good Loan": the loans which are fully paid or the installment are being regularly paid by the customers. 
- What's a "Bad Loan": the loan which the customer defaulted on the loan, and the bank had to "charge it off" from their accounts
### Solution provided
#### Dashboard 1: Summary
- Key Loan Metrics and their delta over time
- Used primarily for tracking loan portfolio health and identify trends to form better business strategies
- Good and loan KPIs: (loan application percentage, loan applications, loan total amount, loan total received amount)
#### Dashboard 2: 
- Monthly trends by issue date, regional analysis by state, loan term analysis, employment duration analysis, loan purpose breakdown, home ownership analysis
#### Dashboard 3:
- It serves as a one-stop solution for users seeking detailed insights into loan portfolio, borrower profiles, and loan performance.
### Business outcome achieved
We were able to ascertain the financial health of the bank, the statistics regarding good and bad loans. Further action would be to figure out how to tackle the rising situation of bad loans and devise policies for better lending strategies.
